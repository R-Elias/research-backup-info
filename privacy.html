Privacy information

This personal application requests only access to its own Google Drive application-data area. It stores encrypted research-server backups there and lists, downloads, verifies, and deletes its backup files to implement recovery and retention.

It does not request access to ordinary Google Drive documents. Backup contents are encrypted by Restic before upload. Google hosts the encrypted data. The operator retains OAuth credentials on the VPS and in protected recovery copies to perform scheduled backups.

Backup data is not sold or used for advertising. The application does not intentionally share it with other users. Expired snapshots are removed according to the configured retention policy.

The owner can revoke access in Google Account settings. Revocation stops future access; deleting the app's hidden data removes backups. Contact for questions or deletion: hell@no.com .
